SIC Decoding Order
• At the UA V receiver, SIC is adopted to decode the superposed uplink signals. Since 
URLLC traffic is latency-critical, the URLLC packet is decoded before the eMBB
packet.
• Hence, the decoding order is:
1. Decode the URLLC signal first;
2. If the URLLC signal is successfully decoded, subtract it from the received 
signal;
3. Decode the eMBB signal from the residual signal.
