UAV-Assisted Multiplexing of eMBB and URLLC
•

We consider a multi-UAV assisted uplink communication network supporting the coexistence of

eMBB and URLLC services.
•

In the considered system, a set of ground users transmit uplink data to aerial access points (UAVs),
which are ABS. Each UAV serves a group of users through uplink wireless access links.

•

To improve spectral efficiency, NOMA is employed so that an eMBB user and a URLLC user may

share the same resource block.
•

At the UAV receiver , successive interference cancellation (SIC) is used to separate the
superposed signals.
